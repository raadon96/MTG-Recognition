The other common layouts (transform, modal_dfc, saga, class, case, mutate, prototype, meld: 3.0% of paper printings) place every detail where a normal card does: on 160 samples, the name, the collector information and power/toughness were all inside today's crops. Traps

  • The catalog isn't what the card shows. A List card is PLST HOP-128, base set size 5,075 in the catalog, but prints HOP 128/169. A prerelease promo PKHM 214p prints KHM 214/285.
  • The year on the card isn't the set's year. Secret Lair prints 2023 to 2026, the catalog says 2019; Time Spiral Remastered prints 2020, the catalog says 2021.
  • The frame doesn't tell the collector style. Retro reprints (The List, Remasters, 30A, Secret Lair) use an old frame with modern details: a 1997-frame card from 2023 prints a collector number, but no base set size.
  • Two-name cards have one name in the catalog. Both printings of Bonecrusher Giant // Stomp hold the full name, the same collector number and the same image.
  • The big name may not be the card's name. On flavor-name cards (2.9%) the large name is the flavor name; the card name is printed small below it, outside the name crop.
  • Other text sits in the collector information crop. The last line of flavor text, the MDFC hint bar, licensing lines (MARVEL), and the grey back-face power/toughness on transform cards.
  • Many printings show exactly the same details. An expansion card and its prerelease, promo-pack and List versions differ only by a stamp: 29% of paper printings can only be told apart by the picture (issue #202).
